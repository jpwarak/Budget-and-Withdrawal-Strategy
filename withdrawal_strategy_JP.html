Sequence-of-Returns Risk (Monte Carlo)

Off by default. Instead of one flat growth rate every year, this draws random year-by-year market returns (the same average, but in a random order) across many simulated retirements, to show how much the ORDER of good and bad years alone can affect outcomes. The deterministic chart/table above is never changed by this.
